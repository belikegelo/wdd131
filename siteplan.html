WDD 131 Website Project Proposal

Website Project Subject

Motorcycle Bucket List: Exploring the Philippines by Motorcycle

Brief Description of the Planned Site Content

This website will be an informational motorcycle travel bucket list featuring several destinations in the Philippines that can be explored by motorcycle. Each destination will include information such as the location, estimated distance and travel time, suggested route, possible stops, things to see and do, motorcycle preparation, items to bring, and an estimated trip budget for fuel, tolls, food, and other expenses. The website will also include an interactive bucket list where users can save their favorite destinations, a motorcycle preparation checklist, and a simple trip budget estimator.

Reason for Choosing This Subject

I chose this subject because I am interested in motorcycles and exploring different places in the Philippines. I want to use the project to combine my interest in motorcycle travel with what I am learning about HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The website will also be useful as a simple planning and research tool for motorcycle trips while allowing me to practice creating an interactive and responsive website.
